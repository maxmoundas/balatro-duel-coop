BALATRO MULTIPLAYER - SETUP
===========================
You need Balatro on Steam (Windows or Mac), launched at least once.
Windows and Mac players can play together.

WINDOWS
1. Close Balatro.
2. Double-click Install.bat (if Windows warns you, click "More info" > "Run anyway").
3. Launch Balatro from Steam and click MULTIPLAYER on the main menu.

MAC
1. Quit Balatro.
2. Open Terminal (Applications > Utilities > Terminal).
3. Type "bash " (with a space), drag install-mac.sh from the "files" folder into the
   Terminal window, and press Enter.
4. Launch Balatro from Steam and click MULTIPLAYER on the main menu.

To uninstall: close Balatro, then double-click Uninstall.bat (Windows), or run
files/uninstall-mac.sh the same way as above (Mac). It removes this mod, then asks whether to
also remove Lovely and Steamodded (keep them if you use other mods).
Your Balatro saves are never touched.

Everything the installer needs is in the "files" folder - leave it next to Install.bat.
