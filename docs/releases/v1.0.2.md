### New
- **Same seed or different seeds.** The host can now choose in the lobby whether everyone plays
  the same seed (same jokers, shops, packs and bosses: the default) or each player gets their own
  random seed. Click **Use different seeds** / **Use same seed** before starting. Works in Duel and
  Co-op.
- **Co-op Endless mode together.** After beating Ante 8 as a team, choose Endless Mode and keep
  playing together: rounds stay in sync and scores stay pooled, just like before the win.

### Fixed
- The "TEAM VICTORY" popup with the Main Menu button no longer stays on screen after you continue
  into Endless mode (the Duel result had the same problem).
- In Co-op Endless mode, players are kept in sync again: nobody moves on to the shop or the next
  blind until everyone is ready.
