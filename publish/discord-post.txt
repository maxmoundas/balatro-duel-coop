I recently finished Completion++ on both the mobile and Steam versions of Balatro, and I've gotten my family hooked on it too. Watching someone else play only goes so far, so I made **Balatro Duel & Co-op**, a mod that adds multiplayer to Balatro.

## Two modes
- **Co-op (2–4 players):** everyone plays their own run on the same seed, with your scores pooled into one shared blind. Beat Ante 8 together.
- **Duel (1v1):** you and your opponent play the same seed side by side and compete for points on every blind.

You play at your own pace, and you can press Tab any time to look at the other players' jokers and deck.

Works on **Windows and Mac** (they can play together) through **Steam friend invites**: no server, no port forwarding. The installer sets everything up for you.

**Download:** <https://github.com/maxmoundas/balatro-duel-coop> (also on [Thunderstore](<https://thunderstore.io/c/balatro/p/MaxMoundas/Balatro_Duel_Coop/>) for r2modman/Gale, [Nexus Mods](<https://www.nexusmods.com/balatro/mods/1009>), and as a [Steam guide](<https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3842026023>))
