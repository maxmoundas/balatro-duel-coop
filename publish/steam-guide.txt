DESCRIPTION (the short summary under the title)
-----
Play Balatro with your Steam friends: 2-4 player Co-op with a shared blind score, or 1v1 Duels on the same seed. Join through Steam friend invites, with no server or port forwarding. Windows and Mac. This guide covers installing, playing and troubleshooting.

SECTION 1 TITLE
-----
What it is
-----
SECTION 1 CONTENT
-----
Balatro Duel & Co-op is a mod that adds multiplayer to Balatro. Each player runs their own copy of the game, so everything plays exactly like the real thing.

[h2]Two modes[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Co-op (2–4 players):[/b] everyone plays their own run on the same seed, but your scores are pooled into one shared blind. Blind requirements scale with the number of players, and if you run out of hands, your teammates can still carry you through. Beat Ante 8 together.
[*][b]Duel (1v1):[/b] you and your opponent play the same seed side by side. Each blind awards a point for the best single hand and a point for beating it in the fewest hands. Outlast your opponent, or outscore them if you both make it to the end.
[/list]

You play at your own pace within each round, and the game keeps everyone in sync between rounds. Press [b]Tab[/b] at any time to look at the other players' jokers, deck and hand levels.

Full rules for each mode: [url=https://github.com/maxmoundas/balatro-duel-coop/blob/main/docs/HOW-IT-PLAYS.md]How multiplayer plays[/url]

[i]Not to be confused with the separate "Balatro Multiplayer" mod. This is an unofficial fan mod and isn't affiliated with LocalThunk or Playstack.[/i]

SECTION 2 TITLE
-----
What you need
-----
SECTION 2 CONTENT
-----
[list]
[*][b]Balatro on Steam, on a Windows PC or a Mac.[/b] Every player needs their own copy, because Steam Family Sharing doesn't let two accounts play the same copy at once. Windows and Mac players can play together.
[*][b]To be Steam friends with whoever is hosting.[/b]
[*][b]Your Steam status set to Online[/b], not Invisible or Offline. Invites and joining go through Steam's friend status.
[*][b]Balatro launched at least once[/b], all the way to the main menu, so its save folder exists.
[/list]

The download is on GitHub, so Steam will show a warning when you click the link. That's normal for any link that leaves Steam.

SECTION 3 TITLE
-----
Install on Windows
-----
SECTION 3 CONTENT
-----
[olist]
[*][b]Download the installer:[/b] [url=https://github.com/maxmoundas/balatro-duel-coop/releases/latest/download/BalatroDuelCoop-Setup.zip]BalatroDuelCoop-Setup.zip[/url] (this link always gives you the latest version).
[*][b]Unzip it:[/b] right-click the zip > Extract All > Extract. Running Install.bat from inside the zip without extracting it won't work.
[*][b]Close Balatro[/b] if it's open.
[*][b]Double-click Install.bat.[/b] If Windows warns you, click "More info" > "Run anyway". It finds Balatro through Steam and installs Lovely (the mod loader), Steamodded (the mod framework) and this mod. If you already have Lovely or Steamodded, it leaves them as they are.
[*][b]Launch Balatro from Steam[/b] as usual. The [b]MULTIPLAYER[/b] button appears on the main menu.
[/olist]

Also available on [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/balatro/mods/1009]Nexus Mods[/url].
-----


SECTION 4 TITLE
-----
Install on Mac
-----
SECTION 4 CONTENT
-----
[olist]
[*][b]Quit Balatro.[/b]
[*][b]Open Terminal[/b] (Applications > Utilities > Terminal), paste this line and press Enter:
[code]curl -fsSL https://github.com/maxmoundas/balatro-duel-coop/releases/latest/download/install-mac.sh | bash[/code]
[*][b]Launch Balatro from Steam[/b] as usual. The [b]MULTIPLAYER[/b] button appears on the main menu.
[/olist]

On Mac, Lovely can't hook into the game the way it does on Windows, so the installer puts a tiny launcher in place of the game's program and keeps the original next to it. Launching from Steam and accepting Steam invites both start the modded game.

[b]If a Balatro update makes the MULTIPLAYER button disappear, run the install line again.[/b]

SECTION 5 TITLE
-----
Play with your friends
-----
SECTION 5 CONTENT
-----
[olist]
[*][b]Host:[/b] click [b]MULTIPLAYER[/b]. Under [b]Duel[/b] or [b]Co-op[/b], click [b]Choose deck & stake[/b] (or [b]Play a challenge[/b]), pick one, then [b]Play[/b].
[*][b]Host:[/b] in the lobby, click [b]Invite Steam Friends[/b], then [b]Invite[/b] next to your friend.
[*][b]Friend:[/b]
[list]
[*][b]Balatro is open:[/b] an invite banner appears in the MULTIPLAYER menu. Click [b]Join[/b].
[*][b]Balatro is closed:[/b] accept the invite in Steam chat. Balatro launches and joins the lobby by itself.
[*][b]No invite shows up:[/b] the host clicks [b]Copy invite code[/b] and sends it to the friend. The friend opens MULTIPLAYER, clicks [b]Paste[/b], then [b]Join[/b].
[/list]
[*][b]Host:[/b] once everyone is in the lobby, click [b]Start Game[/b].
[/olist]

[h2]In game[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Tab[/b] (or clicking the status bar) opens the player viewer: compare yourself with the other players, including their jokers, consumables, vouchers, tags, hand, whole deck, poker hand levels and the score of every blind.
[*][b]F7[/b] toggles the multiplayer status bar.
[*]Your name in multiplayer is your Steam name. To use a different one, set it under MULTIPLAYER > [b]Advanced[/b].
[/list]

SECTION 6 TITLE
-----
Updating and uninstalling
-----
SECTION 6 CONTENT
-----
[h2]Updating[/h2]
When a new version comes out, [b]every player must update[/b]. On Windows, download the zip again from the same link, extract it, and run Install.bat again. On Mac, run the install line again. Either way it replaces the old version of the mod and leaves Lovely and Steamodded as they are.

[h2]Uninstalling[/h2]
[olist]
[*]Close Balatro.
[*][b]Windows:[/b] double-click [b]Uninstall.bat[/b] in the folder you extracted (download and extract the zip again if you deleted it).
[b]Mac:[/b] run this in Terminal:
[code]curl -fsSL https://github.com/maxmoundas/balatro-duel-coop/releases/latest/download/uninstall-mac.sh | bash[/code]
[*]It removes this mod and its settings, then asks whether to also remove Lovely and Steamodded. Answer [b]N[/b] (the default) if you use any other mods. Answer [b]Y[/b] to make Balatro completely unmodded again.
[/olist]

Your Balatro save files are never touched.

SECTION 7 TITLE
-----
If something goes wrong
-----
SECTION 7 CONTENT
-----
[list]
[*][b]"Their game didn't answer through Steam":[/b] check that you're Steam friends, that both of you show as Online, and that the host's lobby is still open. Then try again.
[*][b]The MULTIPLAYER button is missing:[/b] run the installer again. If Balatro shows a Lovely or Steamodded error when it starts, take a screenshot.
[*][b]Mac: Balatro starts without the MULTIPLAYER button:[/b] a Balatro update probably replaced the launcher. Run the install line again.
[*][b]Other mods:[/b] turn off mods that change scoring, like Talisman. They aren't supported yet.
[*][b]Something else:[/b] grab the newest log file from Lovely's log folder:
[list]
[*]Windows: [b]%APPDATA%\Balatro\Mods\lovely\log\[/b] (paste that into the File Explorer address bar)
[*]Mac: [b]~/Library/Application Support/Balatro/Mods/lovely/log/[/b] (in Finder, press Shift+Cmd+G and paste it)
[/list]
[/list]

[b]Still stuck, or found a bug?[/b] Leave a comment on this guide, or [url=https://github.com/maxmoundas/balatro-duel-coop/issues/new/choose]report it on GitHub[/url] and attach that log file.
